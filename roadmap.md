# Roadmap 2026–2036

The roadmap converts the earlier environmental-geography/GIS foundation into a coherent program in **computational environmental geography**. Every phase below is a planning target, not a completed-achievement record.

<div class="phase-grid">
  <div class="card"><div class="eyebrow">Phase 01 · 2026–2027</div><h3>Standardize the foundation</h3><p>Digitize the 2016–2026 data legacy; standardize the academic CV; draft an initial ontology; clarify the doctoral research direction; test NLP annotation on environmental text. Target outputs: data inventory, annotation guideline v0.1, small RAG demo and one review/conceptual paper.</p></div>
  <div class="card"><div class="eyebrow">Phase 02 · 2028–2029</div><h3>Build corpus & core models</h3><p>Create a Vietnamese coastal-environment corpus, NER/RE label schemes and spatial linking through GIS/GeoJSON; run GeoAI pilots in Ben Tre, Tra Vinh and Can Gio. Targets: corpus v1.0, baseline NER, risk maps and 2–3 papers.</p></div>
  <div class="card"><div class="eyebrow">Phase 03 · 2030–2031</div><h3>Doctoral integration & DSS</h3><p>Integrate spatial data, textual data and local knowledge; develop source-grounded RAG/LLM and a decision-support dashboard; evaluate with experts and local stakeholders. Targets: doctoral thesis, dashboard, knowledge graph, core papers and transfer/evaluation SOPs.</p></div>
  <div class="card"><div class="eyebrow">Phase 04 · 2032–2033</div><h3>Regional expansion & standards</h3><p>Extend to the coastal Mekong Delta; standardize label sets, dictionaries and ontology; connect environmental management with agriculture, aquaculture and ecotourism. Targets: open group-level data standards, provincial/VNU-HCM projects, training materials and a young-researcher network.</p></div>
  <div class="card"><div class="eyebrow">Phase 05 · 2034–2036</div><h3>Research leadership</h3><p>Form an Environmental GeoAI & NLP research group/lab, sustain international publishing, train students and transfer systems to local agencies. Targets include a monograph/textbook, additional publications and a coastal-environment data/AI platform.</p></div>
</div>

## 2036 target positioning

**Target specialty:** Computational Environmental Geography — GIS, GeoAI and NLP/LLM for coastal resource and environmental management.

**Target academic contribution:** an integrated spatial–textual–local-knowledge framework; Vietnamese environmental corpus and ontology; coastal Mekong case studies; decision-support models.

**Target practical contribution:** reduce time spent reading and synthesizing environmental files, improve evidence traceability, prioritize field surveys, map risk and support transfer to local management practice.

<div class="notice"><strong>Use in formal dossiers:</strong> planned papers, projects, degrees, research groups and intellectual-property outputs should only move from this roadmap into an “achieved” section after there is formal evidence of acceptance, award, completion, publication or certification.</div>
