# Roadmap

The roadmap describes a forward-looking research program. Future milestones are strategic targets and should not be interpreted as completed achievements.

## Phase sequence

<div class="phase-grid">
  <div class="card"><div class="eyebrow">PHASE 01</div><h3>Consolidate the geoinformatics core</h3><p>Standardize spatial data, WebGIS/WebGeo workflows, environmental datasets, reproducible code, and clear evidence documentation.</p></div>
  <div class="card"><div class="eyebrow">PHASE 02</div><h3>Build environmental simulation capacity</h3><p>Develop landscape-dynamics, CA, ABM, and system-dynamics components for coupled human–environment questions.</p></div>
  <div class="card"><div class="eyebrow">PHASE 03</div><h3>Develop Vietnamese GeoNLP</h3><p>Connect place names, environmental documents, local knowledge, policy evidence, ontologies, and geographic knowledge graphs.</p></div>
  <div class="card"><div class="eyebrow">PHASE 04</div><h3>Integrate GeoAI and spatial agents</h3><p>Combine spatial machine learning, graph reasoning, map-aware tools, geographic memory, and language reasoning.</p></div>
  <div class="card"><div class="eyebrow">PHASE 05</div><h3>Operationalize decision systems</h3><p>Deliver auditable, human-in-the-loop systems for environmental planning, climate adaptation, and spatial decision support.</p></div>
</div>

## Quality targets

- A coherent research identity centered on geoinformatics and environmental systems.
- Reusable code, datasets, methods, and WebGeo/WebGIS components.
- A connected publication sequence rather than unrelated papers.
- Strong separation between verified outputs and proposed work.
- Internationally legible research framing without unsupported ranking claims.
- Increasing integration of simulation, GeoAI, GeoNLP, and spatial agents.
- Transparent evidence chains from geographic observations to model-assisted decisions.
