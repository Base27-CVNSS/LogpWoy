## Research focus

My long-term research theme is **Spatial-Textual Environmental Intelligence for Coastal Resource Management in the Mekong Delta** — a framework connecting environmental geography with geospatial computation and language intelligence.

The research program concentrates on three linked questions:

1. How can satellite/GIS observations, environmental reports and field knowledge be represented in one auditable spatial-textual data architecture?
2. How can GeoAI and Vietnamese NLP/LLM systems extract risks, resources, livelihoods, impacts, adaptations and supporting evidence without losing geographic context?
3. How can these outputs be transformed into transparent maps, knowledge graphs, RAG systems and decision-support dashboards for provincial and regional environmental management?

## Selected research foundations

- Integrated freshwater-resource management on **Ly Son Island** and marine-island resource-position research in **Kien Giang**.
- Climate-change livelihood adaptation around the **Ba Lai sluice/dam system, Ben Tre**.
- GIS integration of a **Sustainable Livelihood Security Index for Phu Quoc Island**.
- Land-use structure change in **Tra Vinh** under climate change and sea-level-rise conditions.
- Sustainable livelihoods linked to conservation in the **Can Gio mangrove biosphere reserve**.
- A 2026–2028 oyster-farming task in Vinh Long is treated on this website as a current/planned transition toward field-data governance, traceability and scientific communication.

## Strategic objective to 2036

Develop an interdisciplinary profile in **computational environmental geography**, with core contributions in spatial-textual-local-knowledge integration, Vietnamese environmental corpora/ontologies, GeoAI case studies for the coastal Mekong Delta, and human-centered decision-support systems.
