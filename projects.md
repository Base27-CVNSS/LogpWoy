# Projects

## Development logic

<div class="flowline">
  <span>OBSERVE</span><b>→</b><span>MODEL</span><b>→</b><span>EXPLAIN</span><b>→</b><span>SIMULATE</span><b>→</b><span>ACT</span>
</div>

Projects should satisfy five requirements:

- <strong>Spatially explicit:</strong> every important variable has a geographic reference or scale.
- <strong>Evidence-linked:</strong> assumptions and outputs remain traceable to data or documented knowledge.
- <strong>Reproducible:</strong> workflows can be rerun and inspected.
- <strong>Interoperable:</strong> GIS, simulation, NLP, and AI components exchange structured data.
- <strong>Decision-oriented:</strong> outputs support comparison, interpretation, or action rather than visualization alone.
