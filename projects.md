## Current / transition task

### 2026–2028 · Commercial oyster-farming model in Vinh Long

<span class="badge plan">CURRENT / PLANNED IN LLKH</span>

The strategic LLKH describes a scientific-secretariat/data-coordination role for a modular floating-raft oyster-farming model designed for climate adaptation and traceability. Its strategic value is the transition toward **field-data governance, digital logs, traceability, dashboard reporting and scientific communication**.
